FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does every member need to use the dashboard?

No. Members can continue voting and joining events entirely inside Discord. The dashboard is mainly designed for alliance leaders.

Is Pro limited to one person?

No. AllianceHQ Pro unlocks Pro features for the selected Discord server.

Where can the subscription be cancelled?

Subscriptions are managed directly through Discord and can be cancelled in Discord subscription settings.

Does AllianceHQ store payment details?

No. Checkout and billing are handled by Discord. AllianceHQ only processes the Discord entitlement that confirms plan access.

Is AllianceHQ tied to one game?

No. AllianceHQ works for alliances, clans, guilds and other coordinated gaming communities.
